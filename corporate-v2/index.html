Welcome to INNOK

Materials that bring design and function together.

Based in Dangjin, South Korea, INNOK works in the decorative materials and trade sector, with a focus on luxury vinyl tile (LVT) flooring, decorative films, and printing & coating.

We understand that every project starts with the right material. Our work is centred on product requirements, practical communication, and connecting materials with business opportunities.
